- **001 Player archetype clustering (UMAP + HDBSCAN), per season**

  - Fine gives 7-9 nested sub-roles (goalscorer vs playmaker; attacking vs defensive full-back).
  - Position is recovered without being a feature - archetypes are football-sensible.
  - A minutes floor (>=900) is essential; otherwise coarse clustering just recovers played-vs-not-played.
  - Coarse via merging fine centroids is stable across seasons; a 2nd independent HDBSCAN was not.
- **002 Align & name player archetypes across seasons**

- **003 Archetype transitions season to season**
  - 358 player-pairs; only 35% keep the same fine archetype year to year.

  - Genuine cross-role switches are rare.
- **004 Points-per-million by archetype**
  - Elite playmakers (24.0) and Elite goalscorers (23.0) are best value despite premium prices.
  - Goalkeepers (21.4) and attacking/ball-playing defenders (20.2) are the cheap value sweet spot.
  - Rotation midfielders (10.7) and defensive/rotation defenders (10.0) are worst value.
- **005 Cluster teams by archetype makeup**
